# Investment Research Best Practices

## Introduction
Successful investment research requires a multi-faceted approach. The Stock Toolbox Web Application is designed to facilitate this by integrating Technical, Fundamental, and Sentiment analysis. This guide outlines best practices for using these tools effectively.

## 1. Technical Analysis (The "When")
**Goal:** Determine the optimal entry or exit points based on price action.

*   **Trend Identification:** Use the **Monthly** and **Weekly** charts to identify the long-term trend. Is the stock in an uptrend, downtrend, or consolidation?
    *   *Best Practice:* Never trade against the long-term trend unless you are executing a specific mean-reversion strategy.
*   **Entry/Exit Timing:** Use the **Daily** chart for precise timing. Look for pullbacks to support levels in an uptrend or rallies to resistance in a downtrend.
*   **Volume Confirmation:** Always verify price movements with volume. A breakout with low volume is suspect; a breakout with high volume confirms conviction.

## 2. Fundamental Analysis (The "Why")
**Goal:** Determine the intrinsic value and financial health of the company.

*   **Valuation Check:** Look at the P/E ratio relative to historical averages and peers. Is the stock cheap or expensive?
*   **Financial Health:** Use the **SEC Filings** tab to check the Balance Sheet. Look for:
    *   **Debt Levels:** Is the debt manageable?
    *   **Cash Flow:** Is the company generating positive Free Cash Flow?
*   **Business Model:** Use the **AI Analysis** feature to get a quick summary of what the company actually does and its competitive advantages (moat).
    *   *Best Practice:* Invest in businesses you understand. If the AI summary is confusing, dig deeper or move on.

## 3. Sentiment Analysis (The "Now")
**Goal:** Understand market psychology and potential catalysts.

*   **News Catalyst:** Use the **News** tab to see what is driving the current price action. Are there earnings surprises, regulatory changes, or product launches?
*   **Sentiment Check:** Use the **AI Sentiment Analysis** to gauge the mood.
    *   *Contrarian Approach:* Extreme positive sentiment often marks a top, while extreme negative sentiment can mark a bottom.
    *   *Momentum Approach:* Rising positive sentiment can confirm a breakout.

## 4. Synthesis: The 3-Step Validation
Before making a decision, ensure alignment across all three pillars:

1.  **Fundamentals:** The company is solid and reasonably valued.
2.  **Technicals:** The chart shows a favorable risk/reward ratio (e.g., buying at support).
3.  **Sentiment:** There are no immediate "red flag" news items, or the bad news is already priced in.

## 5. Risk Management
*   **Diversification:** Do not put all capital into one ticker. Use the **Ticker Lists** feature to manage a diversified watchlist.
*   **Continuous Monitoring:** Markets change. Regularly check the **News** and **SEC Filings** for your holdings to ensure the investment thesis remains valid.
