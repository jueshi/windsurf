# Structured Investment Workflow

## Overview
This document defines a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for analyzing a stock using the Stock Toolbox Web Application. Follow this workflow to ensure consistency and thoroughness in your research.

## Phase 1: Screening & Discovery
**Objective:** Identify potential candidates.

1.  **Add to Watchlist:** Identify a ticker of interest (from social media, screener, or news) and add it to your "Inbox" or "Watchlist" in the application sidebar.
2.  **Initial Glance:** Click the ticker to load the dashboard.
3.  **Price Check:** Look at the Daily chart.
    *   *Decision Gate:* Is the chart completely broken (e.g., massive gap down)? If yes, *Reject* (unless looking for deep value). If it looks stable or trending up, *Proceed*.

## Phase 2: Fundamental Deep Dive
**Objective:** Verify business quality.

1.  **Switch to Fundamentals Tab:**
2.  **Review Key Metrics:** Check Market Cap (size), P/E (valuation), and Dividend Yield (income).
3.  **Run AI Analysis:** Click "Run AI Analysis" to generate a business summary.
    *   *Question:* Do I understand this business? Is it profitable?
4.  **Check Financials (SEC Filings):** Switch to the "SEC Filings" tab.
    *   Open the latest 10-Q.
    *   Review the Income Statement: Is Revenue growing?
    *   Review the Cash Flow: Is "Net Cash from Operating Activities" positive?
    *   *Decision Gate:* If financials are deteriorating without a clear turnaround plan, *Reject*. Otherwise, *Proceed*.

## Phase 3: Sentiment & Catalyst Check
**Objective:** Assess external risks and timing.

1.  **Switch to News Tab:**
2.  **Scan Headlines:** Look for the last 5-10 articles.
3.  **Run AI Sentiment:** Click "Analyze Sentiment" to get a summary.
    *   *Check:* Are there pending lawsuits, CEO departures, or accounting scandals?
    *   *Decision Gate:* If there is an unresolved "existential threat", *Wait* or *Reject*. If news is routine or positive, *Proceed*.

## Phase 4: Technical Entry Planning
**Objective:** Determine exactly when to buy.

1.  **Switch to Chart Tab:**
2.  **Multi-Timeframe Analysis:**
    *   **Monthly:** Check the 5-year trend.
    *   **Weekly:** Check for major support/resistance levels.
    *   **Daily:** Look for a specific setup (e.g., Bull Flag, Support Bounce, Breakout).
3.  **Set Alerts (Mental or External):** Decide on a specific buy price.
    *   "I will buy if price closes above $X."
    *   "I will buy if price pulls back to $Y."

## Phase 5: Execution & Monitoring
1.  **Execute Trade:** (Performed in your brokerage account, outside this app).
2.  **Update List:** Move the ticker from "Watchlist" to "Portfolio" list in the sidebar.
3.  **Weekly Review:**
    *   Review the Weekly chart every weekend.
    *   Check for new SEC Filings or major News events using the app.
